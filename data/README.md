Download `cardio_train.csv` from the [Cardiovascular Disease Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sulianova/cardiovascular-disease-dataset) and place it in this folder.

The data are not included here. The original file has 70,000 rows and uses semicolons. Age is recorded in days, height in centimeters, weight in kilograms, and blood pressure in mmHg. The `cardio` column is the disease label, with 0 for absence and 1 for presence.

Keep the original units. Do not convert age to years before running the script. The original `gender` codes are 1 and 2; cholesterol and glucose use categories 1, 2, and 3. Smoking, alcohol, and activity use 0 and 1.

The download page gives the dataset's terms. The repository's MIT license applies to the code.
